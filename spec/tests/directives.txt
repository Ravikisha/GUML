Directives: `page` and its metadata, `type`, `state`, `data`.

:::: page metadata becomes document metadata
`page Name` names a component. A document also needs a title, a language and a direction — without
`lang`, assistive technology guesses pronunciation.
--- guml
page Docs title="Read the docs" description="How GUML works." lang=en-GB dir=rtl
p Body.
--- ast
page=Docs title="Read the docs" description="How GUML works." lang="en-GB" dir="rtl"
p content="Body."
--- html
<html lang="en-GB" dir="rtl">
<title>Read the docs</title>
<meta name="description" content="How GUML works." />
--- diagnostics

:::: a pipe-separated state is an enumerated domain
The first value is the initial one. The domain is what lets `tabs` render a segmented control with no
options written by hand.
--- guml
page P
state filter=all|open|done
tabs filter
--- ast
page=P
state filter domain=all|open|done
tabs text="filter"
--- diagnostics

:::: a def expands to its body with arguments substituted
A compile-time macro, not a runtime component: by codegen the tree holds no trace of one, which is what
lets a `def` work in every backend with no per-backend support.
--- guml
page P
state total=0
def stat label value
  card sm center
    h {label}
    metric {value}
stat "Revenue" {total}
--- ast
page=P
state total
card mod=sm mod=center
  h content="Revenue"
  metric content="{total}"
--- diagnostics

:::: a def may call another def
--- guml

--- ast

::::

:::: a def inherits its level from its body
A body of markup is core. There is no `level` on a `def` because there is nothing to declare.
level: core
--- guml
page P
def panel title
  card {title}
    p Body.
panel "Hi"
--- ast
page=P
card text="Hi"
  p content="Body."
--- diagnostics
