Lines, indentation, and the prose-versus-structure decision.

The rule a second implementation is most likely to get wrong is the last one: whether the remainder of
a line is a label plus modifiers or free prose depends on the tag's kind in the registry, and no
tokeniser can decide it alone.

:::: a page directive names the document
--- guml
page Counter
--- ast
page=Counter
--- diagnostics
::::

:::: children are lines with a strictly greater indent
--- guml
page P
card A
  p One
    p Two
  p Three
card B
--- ast
page=P
card text="A"
  p content="One"
    p content="Two"
  p content="Three"
card text="B"
--- diagnostics
::::

:::: a one-space step is still a child
Ragged as it looks. The indent stack decides depth, not the space count — an implementation that
guessed "these are siblings" would change the tree.
--- guml
page P
card A
  p One
   p Two
--- ast
page=P
card text="A"
  p content="One"
    p content="Two"
--- diagnostics
::::

:::: a tab is an error, not an indent unit
--- guml
page P
card A
	p One
--- diagnostics

:::: an indented line with no parent is an error
--- guml
page P
  card A
--- diagnostics
GUML0011 2
::::

:::: comment lines are dropped and affect nothing
--- guml
page P
// a note
card A
  // another
  p One
--- ast
page=P
card text="A"
  p content="One"
--- diagnostics
::::

:::: a text tag takes its whole remainder as prose
--- guml
page P
p Press the buttons to change the value.
--- ast
page=P
p content="Press the buttons to change the value."
--- diagnostics
::::

:::: prose is never reflowed or respaced
Runs of spaces inside prose are content. Collapsing them would be silently editing the document.
--- guml
page P
p Two  spaces   inside
--- ast
page=P
p content="Two  spaces   inside"
--- diagnostics
::::

:::: prose containing an equals sign stays prose
An `=` is not enough to make a line structured. This parsed as a positional, an attribute `x=1`, and
four discarded words — deleting most of the sentence and emitting an invalid DOM prop.
--- guml
page P
p Set x=1 to enable the flag.
--- ast
page=P
p content="Set x=1 to enable the flag."
--- diagnostics
::::

:::: a registry attribute on a text tag is structured
The other direction. `strike` is a `text` attribute, so the registry — not the presence of `=` — is
what distinguishes this from the case above.
--- guml
page P
type T {id, title, done:bool}
data rows:T[] GET /api/r
list rows
  text {title} strike={done}
--- ast
page=P
type T {id:string, title:string, done:bool}
data rows T[] GET /api/r
list text="rows"
  text bind={title} strike={done}
--- diagnostics
::::

:::: a container's first quoted positional is its title and `|` is its prose
--- guml
page P
card "Ship in minutes" | Describe the page, get a deployable build.
--- ast
page=P
card text="Ship in minutes" content="Describe the page, get a deployable build."
--- diagnostics
::::

:::: an action takes the rest of the line
--- guml
page P
state count=0
btn Decrement ghost >count--
--- ast
page=P
state count
btn text="Decrement" mod=ghost action="count--"
--- diagnostics
::::

:::: a modifier written after an action is swallowed by it
Which is why the action must come last. The compiler reports the common case rather than silently
losing the modifier.
--- guml
page P
state count=0
btn Go >count++ primary
--- diagnostics
