Directives: `page` and its metadata, `type`, `state`, `data`.

:::: page metadata becomes document metadata
`page Name` names a component. A document also needs a title, a language and a direction — without
`lang`, assistive technology guesses pronunciation.
--- guml
page Docs title="Read the docs" description="How GUML works." lang=en-GB dir=rtl
p Body.
--- ast
page=Docs title="Read the docs" description="How GUML works." lang="en-GB" dir="rtl"
p content="Body."
--- html
<html lang="en-GB" dir="rtl">
<title>Read the docs</title>
<meta name="description" content="How GUML works." />
--- diagnostics

:::: a def expands to its body with arguments substituted
A compile-time macro, not a runtime component: by codegen the tree holds no trace of one, which is what
lets a `def` work in every backend with no per-backend support.

This case was written as `def stat` and had to be renamed when `stat` became a builtin in 0.2. Worth
recording rather than quietly fixing: adding a tag to the vocabulary is *not* purely additive while
`def` exists, because a document that defined its own component of that name now gets `GUML0093`. That
is the acceptable version of the failure — loud, at compile time, with the name in the message — but it
is a real cost of growing the vocabulary, and `spec/STABILITY.md` now says so.
--- guml
page P
state total=0
def kpi label value
  card sm center
    h {label}
    metric {value}
kpi "Revenue" {total}
--- ast
page=P
state total
card mod=sm mod=center
  h content="Revenue"
  metric content="{total}"
--- diagnostics

:::: a slot receives the call's children
What lets a `def` wrap content rather than only produce it. The children are resolved in the *caller's*
scope, so a binding in them means what it meant where the call was written.
--- guml
page P
def panel title
  card {title}
    slot
panel "Settings"
  p First.
  p Second.
--- ast
page=P
card text="Settings"
  p content="First."
  p content="Second."
--- diagnostics
::::

:::: children with no slot are an error
Dropping them silently is what invariant 3 forbids.
--- guml
